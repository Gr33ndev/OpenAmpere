# Code of Conduct

Deutsch: Wir erwarten einen respektvollen, freundlichen Umgang miteinander, egal ob jemand programmiert, Fehler meldet oder nur eine Frage hat. Es gelten die Regeln unten.

OpenAmpere follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere someone represents the project.

## In short

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are owners of a solar system, not developers, and many write in German. Both are welcome.
- Criticise ideas and code, never people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, or publishing other people's private data (addresses, serial numbers, access keys, screenshots of private chats).
- Do not use the project to advertise products or services.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, contact the maintainer privately using the address in the [imprint](https://gr33ndev.github.io/OpenAmpere/impressum.html). Reports are handled confidentially. German or English are both fine.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews every report and may, depending on the case, ask someone to change their behaviour, remove comments, or block someone from the project, as described in the [enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
